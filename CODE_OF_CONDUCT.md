# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Critique code, not
people.

## The standard

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That document is the authoritative text; it is not reproduced here so it
cannot drift out of date.

In practice, the behaviour that gets someone removed from this project is:
harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing others'
private information, and persistent disruption after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer via
[GitHub](https://github.com/vedantnimbarte) or through the
[Security tab](https://github.com/vedantnimbarte/kortex-memory/security) if
you would rather it stay off the public record.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will not act on a report
without talking to the people involved, unless the situation is urgent.
